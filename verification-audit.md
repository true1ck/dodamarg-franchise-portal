## High-confidence mismatches

These are the strongest public mismatches I found:

- ClearDekho: public materials point to investment at 20 lacs and above, not ₹6L.
- Specsmakers: official franchise page points to roughly ₹45–50L FOCO, not ₹8L.
- Amul: official franchise page says no royalty, which conflicts with the sheet’s royalty assumptions.
- Jockey: public franchise signals are far above ₹9L.
- FirstCry: public franchise signals are far above ₹10L.
- Pizza Galleria: official franchise page says franchise fee of 7 lacs + GST and total setup around 30 lacs, not ₹9L.
- Giani’s: official franchise material says ₹12–18L or ₹13–16L depending on format, not ₹9L.
- Thickshake Factory: official page says budget starting from 30 lakhs, not ₹8L.
- Chicago Pizza: official deck shows ₹7,00,000 fee, 6% royalty, and much higher setup costs than the sheet suggests.
- Jumboking: official FAQ/blog material points to much higher investment and a 6% + GST royalty.

## What this means

Treat the sheet as a brainstorming draft, not a factual market list. If you want, I can next turn this into a clean correction pass:

- keep only the brands that have a real public partner/franchise path,
- replace every mismatched investment figure with the public range, and
- flag anything that should be deleted because it is not a franchise model at all.

Numeric payback is shown only where the brand's own capital figure is firm — see the `Quote dependent` rows for everything else.

**27 candidates were checked and excluded** as not being a real, currently-verifiable franchise/partner program (not a rejection of the brand — just no public capex-and-terms route a first-time applicant could act on):
